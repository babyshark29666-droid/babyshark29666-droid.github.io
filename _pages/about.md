# Publications

**Dynamic truncation and the Berger–Boos corrected h–function method for optimal exact Poisson confidence intervals with unknown backgrounds**<br>
Lu, H., & **Zhao, S.**<br>
*Manuscript under review at Computational Statistics.*

Research on exact Poisson inference for weak signals and unknown backgrounds, combining dynamic truncation, Berger–Boos correction and the h-function. The study compares interval coverage and efficiency, with an application to KARMEN neutrino detection data.

# Research Experience

### Exact Poisson inference with unknown backgrounds
*Research member · September 2025–April 2026*

- Investigated exact inference for rare-event data, with application contexts in high-energy physics and biomedical detection.
- Participated in research on dynamic truncation using Poisson quantiles, Berger–Boos correction and the h-function.
- Compared initial, one-time improved and optimal exact confidence intervals across Bayesian, IM and NIM methods through simulation studies.

### Multidimensional health risks in middle-aged and older adults
*Team lead · March–May 2025*

- Led topic selection, research framework design and modeling strategy using Random Forest and XGBoost feature selection, fixed-effects models and GEE.
- Constructed 6,800+ modeling samples from 21,000+ CHARLS longitudinal records across three waves.
- Identified 12 key frailty predictors from 26 indicators and examined five dimensions of risk: psychological health, physical activity, economic conditions, cognitive function and healthcare services.
- Analyzed age-specific shifts in dominant frailty factors to inform stratified intervention and community health planning.

# Robotics Projects

**Xinbairen Robotics — Control Algorithms Department**<br>
*Reinforcement Learning Assistant Engineer Intern · June–September 2026*

### Cartesian-space admittance control for a six-axis manipulator

- Built a mass–damping–stiffness admittance model to convert external forces into Cartesian position and velocity corrections for contact tasks, surface following and human–robot interaction.
- Combined these corrections with linear and circular trajectories, with displacement, velocity and torque limits in the control loop.
- Deployed the algorithm from MuJoCo to an ELFIN10 robot, aligning the kinematic model, control timing and interfaces, and completing hardware parameter calibration and validation through CODESYS and TCP.

### Joint-space impedance control for a six-axis manipulator

- Computed impedance torques from joint position and velocity errors, combining trajectory tracking with compliant responses to external obstruction.
- Used Pinocchio-based dynamics modeling for gravity, Coriolis and friction compensation, with soft start, torque limits and connection-loss protection.
- Completed simulation-to-hardware deployment and trajectory validation, aligning joint order, zero offsets, units and torque directions for CST torque control.
